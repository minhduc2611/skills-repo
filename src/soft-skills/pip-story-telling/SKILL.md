## Definition

[Concept](story-structure/concept.md): Stories shape how we see ourselves and the world around us. Frame your work as an epic adventure.
[Explore](story-structure/explore.md): Stories help us navigate confusing, unknown and changing situations. Make a map for your way ahead.
[Character](story-structure/character.md): Stories connect us to other people. Show us why we can trust you.
[Function](story-structure/function.md): Stories are more effective than facts or opinions by themselves. Put your stories to work.
[Structure](story-structure/structure.md): Stories carry us along thanks to a few basic patterns. Make your ideas flow in a story-ish way.
[Style](story-structure/style.md): Stories carry useful information. Make sure people remember yours.
[Organise](story-structure/organize.md): Stories work, so long as you use them well. Plan how you should tell yours.
[Recipe](story-structure/recipe.md): Stories can change the world. See which problems you can solve by combining different story tactics.
